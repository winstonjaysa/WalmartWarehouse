Backroom · a night of receiving & stocking

A Walmart Canada store · overnight

What happens in the backroom while the store sleeps

The truck arrives, boxes come off, get sorted by department, and are stocked on the shelves before morning. Scroll to follow one night, step by step.

Scroll, swipe or use the arrows ›

Step 1 · Truck at the dock

Unloading the trailer

A Walmart trailer is backed into the dock door. Floor-loaded cases are lifted by hand, one at a time, onto the head of the roller. The Stocking 2 team (backroom / unload) does this work.

Trailers carry a placard: FDS, CP, ADS or MP. MP = Meat & Produce. Others are shown by what they carry (ambient DC freight, fresh dairy/frozen).

Step 2 · Breaking down skids

Mixed skids, stretch-wrapped

Some freight comes on pallets (skids) wrapped in plastic film, about 1.9 m tall on a 48×40 in pallet. A worker pulls each skid out with a pallet jack, cuts the wrap, and a partner sends the cases onto the roller. Empty pallets are stacked by the wall.

Step 3 · The roller

A gravity roller down the middle

A skate-wheel conveyor on caster stands runs down the middle of the backroom. It slopes slightly, so boxes roll on their own and queue up where a sorter is waiting.

Step 4 · Sorting by department

Every box goes to its department skid

Sorters stand along the roller and read each box's department number, then stack it on that department's skid: cosmetics, health & beauty, dry grocery, paper, pets and more.

Step 5 · To the sales floor

One person per skid, more for big ones

The overnight team (Stocking 3) pulls each finished skid to its aisle with a pallet jack. A small skid like Dept 46 · Cosmetics needs one person; a big Dry Grocery skid gets a helper.

Step 6 · Overnight stocking

Filling the holes

At the aisle, cases come off the skid and the product goes into the empty spots on the shelf. By opening, the shelves are full.

Frozen & dairy

Milk and eggs, straight to the cooler

Frozen & dairy truck: 5 milk skids (9 crates per layer, 4–6 layers) + wrapped egg pallet go straight to the cooler.

Step 7 · DSD receiving

Vendors deliver direct

Some suppliers bring product themselves (direct store delivery). The vendor pulls the skid in, the DSD associate checks it with the handheld scanner, then signs the invoice at the receiving desk.

DSD · counting

Every wrap comes off

Receiver unwraps every DSD skid to count cases against the invoice. The beverage vendor brings the skids in by pallet jack; the receiver cuts all the stretch wrap off, counts with the handheld, then the skid goes to the backroom.

Step 7 · DSD receiving

A snack vendor backs in

The tractor-trailer reverses slowly to its door. Once the doors are open, the vendor pulls a skid out to be checked in the same way.

Pharmacy

Sealed pharmacy totes

Pharmacy orders arrive as a stretch-wrapped pallet of sealed McKesson-style totes (3 per layer, 2 or 3 layers) with a few cartons on top and the delivery memo on the film. An associate pulls it to the pharmacy with a pallet jack, cuts the wrap, counts the totes against the memo and signs.

Morning pre-op

Baler, compactor, cardboard

Before using them, the baler and compactor get a safety check. Then broken-down cardboard goes into the baler.

  • Baler: door, gate and emergency stop work
  • Compactor: chute door closes, area clear
  • Wave check done
  • Aisles and fire exits clear
  • Pallet jacks and scanners ready

Step 8 · Omni / OGP

Online grocery pickup

The OMNI customer fulfillment team picks online orders into totes and stages them on shelves (chilled and frozen in their own cabinets) until the customer arrives.

Step 8 · Fashion

Apparel goes on Z-racks

Clothing comes in cartons. It is hung on rolling Z-racks, then taken out to the fashion area.

Step 8 · Claims & returns

Damaged and returned items

Damaged cases and customer returns are scanned and kept in the claims cage until they are sent back or written off.

Upstairs

The 1st floor: offices and staff rooms

Up the stairs from the backroom: the training room, lunch room, sick room and cleaning closet, the managers' and personnel offices, and the division shelving.

The whole night

Trucks in, skids out

Trailers unload at the docks, boxes roll down the middle, and every skid ends up in the cooler, the backroom or on the sales floor.
